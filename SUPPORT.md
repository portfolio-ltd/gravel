# Getting help

- **Questions and ideas** — [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/portfolio-ltd/gravel/discussions).
  Search first; someone may have asked already.
- **Bugs** — [open an issue](https://github.com/portfolio-ltd/gravel/issues/new/choose)
  with steps to reproduce and your Gravel version.
- **Security issues** — never in public; see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Gravel is maintained by volunteers, so please be patient — and if you can,
answer someone else's question while you wait.
